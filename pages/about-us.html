Who we are

We are a collective of Christians who believe in the good news of the gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ.

As an extension of the love of the Lord, we are a registered charity, supporting those who need help around the world. We believe love is practical, and that's what our missions are: love in action.

Our name

Our name comes from Jesus' call to His first disciples, who were fishermen by trade. We count it a privilege to share the love of God with all people.

{% include scripture.html text="And he saith unto them, Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men." reference="Matthew 4:19" url="https://biblehub.com/matthew/4-19.htm" %}

What we do

We are serving people all around the world as an extension of the love of God to all people. Our current work includes partnering with a pastor in Pakistan to send children to school, and sharing testimonies of what the Lord has done in people's lives.
